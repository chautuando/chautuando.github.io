title: "Cryptocurrency Prediction Based on Social Media"
excerpt: "Developed time-series forecasting models (ARIMA, Prophet, LSTM, GRU) enhanced by sentiment analysis of 40,000+ cryptocurrency news articles."
collection: portfolio
---

* **Data Engineering**: Scraped and processed a dataset of 40,000+ cryptocurrency news articles from Telegram channels using BeautifulSoup, Selenium, and Telegram API.
* **Sentiment Analysis**: Implemented Transformer-based models (BERT, RoBERTa, DistilBERT) to classify market sentiment.
* **Predictive Modeling**: Developed and evaluated time-series forecasting models (ARIMA, Prophet, LSTM, GRU), achieving a 0.09 MAPE using LSTM and GRU architectures enhanced by sentiment features.
