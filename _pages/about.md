I am an Applied Mathematics graduate with a strong interest in stochastic processes, numerical methods, and their applications in Finance and AI. I recently graduated from the **Budapest University of Technology and Economics** as the Valedictorian of the faculty. In September 2026, I will be starting my Master's degree in Mathematics and Applications at the **University of Paris Dauphine - PSL**, supported by the prestigious PGSM Scholarship (FSMP).

Currently, I work at **FiinGroup** as a Middle Credit Risk Modeling specialist, where I develop and validate credit risk models and AI-driven recommendation systems. Previously, I was a Data Scientist Intern at **BKK (Budapest Transport Center)**.

My research interests include:
* **Stochastic Processes & Probability**: Markov chains mixing times, stochastic modeling.
* **Epidemic Modeling**: Dynamical analysis of SIS/SIR models with applications in insurance.
* **Financial Engineering**: Credit risk modeling, time-series forecasting, and sentiment analysis in cryptocurrency markets.
* **AI & Machine Learning**: LLMs, Graph Neural Networks, and Natural Language Processing.
