 I recently obtained my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics with Excellent degree from the [**Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME)**](https://www.bme.hu/). In September 2026, I will be starting my M1 Mathématiques et Applications, majored in Advanced Mathematics at the [**Université Paris Dauphine - PSL**](https://dauphine.psl.eu/en/), supported by the prestigious PGSM Scholarship (FSMP).

 I am an Applied Mathematics graduate with a strong interest in stochastic processes, numerical methods, and their applications in Finance and AI.

Currently, I work at [**FiinGroup Joint Stock Company**](https://fiingroup.vn/en) as a Credit Risk Modeling specialist, where I develop and validate credit risk models. Previously, I was a Data Scientist Intern at [**Budapesti Közlekedési Központ - BKK (Budapest Transport Center)**](https://www.bkk.hu/).

My research interests include: neural network for PDE, markov chains mixing times, stochastic control, reinforcement learning.
